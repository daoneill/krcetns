Curriculum & Learning Programmes

We combine the National Primary Curriculum with Ethical Education.

Ethical Education ("Learn Together")

Instead of single-faith instruction, our pupils explore moral education, equality, human rights, environmental stewardship, and world belief systems.

STEM & Digital Literacy

Hands-on science experiments, coding workshops, interactive whiteboards, and mathematics problem-solving integrated daily.

Specialised Autism Classes

Our two autism classes feature specialised sensory rooms, learning areas, speech & language integration, and high ANA support ratios.

Hot School Meals Scheme

Every pupil receives a complimentary hot, freshly prepared lunch daily (with vegetarian, vegan, and allergy-friendly options available).

Green Schools Initiative

Pupil-led committees focus on recycling, biodiversity gardens, energy conservation, and sustainable travel to school.
