Our Educational Foundation

The Four Educate Together Pillars

Our school operates under the four core principles of the Educate Together charter, ensuring a supportive environment for every student.

Equality-Based

All children have equal access to school life, and all religious, cultural, and social backgrounds are equally respected and celebrated.

Co-Educational

Boys and girls learn together in a balanced environment, fostering mutual respect, friendships, and equality from early childhood.

Child-Centred

Our teaching methodology prioritizes individual learning needs, curiosity, and well-being, putting the child at the heart of education.

Democratically Run

Parents, staff, and pupils active partnership forms the cornerstone of our school governance, student council, and community life.

Principal's Welcome

Nurturing Curious Minds & Inclusive Values

On behalf of our dedicated staff, pupils, and school community, I am delighted to welcome you to Killester Park Educate Together National School. Opened in 2019, our school is built on the core Educate Together ethos: equality-based, co-educational, child-centred, and democratically run.

We deliver the full Irish National Primary Curriculum alongside our Ethical Education program ("Learn Together"). Whether in mainstream classrooms or our specialized autism classes, we empower every child to shine.
