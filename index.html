Principal's Welcome

Welcome to Killester Park ETNS

It is my great pleasure to welcome you to Killester Park Educate Together National School.

At Killester Park ETNS, we want every child who comes through our doors to feel known, valued and that they truly belong. We are incredibly fortunate to have a dedicated and caring staff team who take the time to know our children well – their strengths, their interests, their personalities and their individual needs. The relationships built between our children, staff and families are at the heart of our school and help to create the warm, welcoming and inclusive community of which we are so proud.

As an Educate Together school, we are fully committed to the Educate Together ethos and to providing an education that is equality-based, child-centred, co-educational and democratically run. We celebrate the diversity of our school community and want every child and family to feel represented, respected and included. We teach the Primary Curriculum set by the Department of Education and Youth and the Learn Together Curriculum, Educate Together’s ethical education curriculum. Through Learn Together, children explore equality, values, belief systems, ethics and social justice, while developing respect for themselves, others and the diverse world around them.

We have high expectations and high standards for every pupil. We endeavour to support all children to reach their full potential, and we are committed to providing high-quality teaching and learning that challenges, supports and inspires our pupils. We want our children to develop strong foundations in their learning while also becoming confident, curious and independent learners who take pride in themselves and their achievements.

We want our pupils to experience a rich and happy primary school journey, with opportunities to develop friendships, discover new interests, express their creativity, have their voices heard and contribute meaningfully to school life and society. Above all, we want children to leave Killester Park ETNS with happy memories, a strong sense of self and the confidence to take their next steps.

We also recognise that a successful school is built through strong partnerships with families and the wider community. We greatly value the contribution of our parents and guardians and look forward to continuing to work together to make our school the very best it can be for every child.

I am very proud to be Principal of Killester Park ETNS and excited about the future of our growing and developing school.

Whether you are an existing member of our school community or a family considering joining us, I hope our website gives you a sense of the warmth, ambition and community spirit that make our school such a special place.

Laura Quinn Principal Killester Park Educate Together National School
Our Educational Foundation

The Four Educate Together Pillars

Our school operates under the four core principles of the Educate Together charter, ensuring a supportive environment for every student.

Equality-Based

All children have equal access to the school and no one religion or worldview is given priority over another within the school. This applies to both enrolment and school life.

Co-Educational

All children are encouraged to explore their full range of abilities and are provided with equal opportunities regardless of their gender or identity.

Child-Centred

Our child-centred approach means that we put children at the heart of all policies and practices and involve them in decision-making where appropriate.

Democratically Run

We run schools on a democratic basis, encouraging active participation by parents and students in the daily life of the school whilst positively affirming the professional role of the teachers.
