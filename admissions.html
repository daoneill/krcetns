Requirement: Applicants must have a diagnosis of Autism with a recommendation for an Autism Class.
Late Applications

Applications made after 12th February 2027 will be considered late applications. The school will consider and issue decisions on late applications in accordance with the school's admission policy.

Offer Acceptance Period

Applicants must confirm acceptance of an offer of admission within 7 days. Failure to accept an offer within the prescribed period may result in the offer being withdrawn.

Autism Class Wait List Validity

Under the Education (Admission to Schools) Act 2018, the waiting list for the Special Class (Autism) is valid only for the school year for which admission is sought. If a place does not become available, a new application must be submitted for the following year.
