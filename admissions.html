Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common admissions and enrollment queries.

Is Killester Park Educate Together a fee-paying school?

No. Killester Park is a state-funded National Primary School operating under the Department of Education. Tuition is entirely free.

Do children wear a school uniform?
No. In line with the Educate Together philosophy, we do not have a school uniform. Children wear practical, comfortable everyday clothes and runners suitable for indoor and outdoor play.
How are places allocated if a class is oversubscribed?
Places are allocated strictly according to the criteria set out in our ratified Annual Admission Policy (available to download in our Policy Center), giving priority to siblings of current pupils and age criteria.
What are the admission requirements for the Autism Class?
Admission to our specialized Autism classes requires a formal psychological assessment diagnosing Autism Spectrum Disorder (ASD) along with a recommendation for placement in a special class setting within a primary school.
