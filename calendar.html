Academic Calendar Notes & Contingency Arrangements

  • Provisional Dates: All dates are provisional and subject to Department of Education directives.
  • Early Closing on Half Days: There will be early closing (12:00 PM) on the last day prior to Winter, Spring, and Summer Breaks (December 22, March 19, and June 25), but regular school hours apply on the last day prior to mid-term breaks (October and February).
  • Easter Contingency Arrangement: Where contingency arrangements are required, a primary school authority may reduce the length of the Easter Break by remaining open up to and including Wednesday 24 March 2027.
