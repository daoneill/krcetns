Location & Public Transport

Conveniently located on Collins Avenue East in Killester, Dublin 5.

DART Train Station

Killester DART Station is just a short 5-minute walk from our school entrance, serving coastal Dublin routes.

Dublin Bus Routes

Frequent bus services (routes 28, 31, 32, 42, 43) stop along Howth Road and Malahide Road nearby.

Active Travel & Parking

Covered bicycle racks are available on campus for pupils and staff. Off-street drop-off zones operate during morning assembly.
